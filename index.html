About

Filip Hantson works on time-aware data systems that preserve what was known when decisions were made. Currently focused on Cryptomnia (the memory layer for crypto risk and attribution) and Pod-OS at PointOfData. Based in Liberty Lake, WA.

Projects

Cryptomnia

Memory layer for crypto. Stores risk scores, attributions, and claims as time-stamped Event Objects so you can prove what was known at the moment a decision was made. Includes auditable forgetting and full provenance. Built on Pod-OS.

cryptomnia.com

PointOfData / Pod-OS

Time-aware multigraph database where every fact is an Event Object carrying its embedding, typed fields, validity interval, source, and confidence. Designed so AI and compliance systems can answer as-of and point-in-time questions instead of operating on last-write-wins snapshots.

pointofdata.ai
